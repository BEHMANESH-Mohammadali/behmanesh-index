# Behmanesh Structural Index (BSI)

**Behmanesh Structural Index (BSI)** is an original, proprietary framework developed by Mohammadali Behmanesh for the structured analysis of intellectual content.

BSI approaches intellectual work as a connected structure of claims, reasoning, assumptions, evidence, conceptual relationships, and analytical choices—rather than as a collection of conclusions alone.

## Purpose

BSI is intended to support rigorous, context-sensitive examination of intellectual content. At a general level, it considers dimensions such as:

- structural and argumentative coherence;
- analytical depth;
- relationships between claims, evidence, and inference;
- epistemic integrity and uncertainty;
- contextual and temporal considerations; and
- the contribution and limitations of the examined content.

BSI is an analytical framework, not a universal ranking system, benchmark leaderboard, or substitute for domain expertise. Its application depends on the authorized framework materials and the specific analytical context.
